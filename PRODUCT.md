# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primary audiences:

- Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating backend / DevOps / systems candidates.
- Potential collaborators and FOSS peers looking for systems-minded builders.
- Prospective clients or freelance leads seeking backend infrastructure work.

They arrive looking for proof of technical depth, range, and taste. Success means they leave with a clear sense of who Samarth is, what he builds, and how to start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

A portfolio-resume that communicates a backend/systems developer's identity and work. It exists to replace a generic CV with a designed point of view: someone who learns by building, cares about infrastructure and browser-native tech, and treats a personal site as a system in its own right.

## Brand Personality

Warm, mechanical, opinionated.

- **Warm:** human, approachable, not a cold enterprise tool.
- **Mechanical:** systems thinking, terminal-native workflow, documented structures.
- **Opinionated:** deliberate choices, a strong point of view, no generic filler.

## Anti-references

- SaaS-cream / beige-muted-slate generic dev portfolios.
- Over-engineered motion that sacrifices usability or gated content.
- Heavy editorial-magazine aesthetics (display-serif overload, broadsheet grids).
- Default "AI-made" scaffolding: repeated tiny uppercase eyebrows, identical card grids, gradient text, ghost-card shadows, oversized border radii.

## Design Principles

1. The site is a system, not a slideshow — every section should feel like a documented component.
2. Motion serves readability; never gate content behind a transition.
3. Personality comes from type, color, and structure, not from decorative gimmicks.
4. Proof over claim: show real projects, real stack choices, real research.
5. Respect the visitor's preferences: reduced motion, contrast, and keyboard access are first-class.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.1 AA.
- Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Ensure keyboard navigability and visible focus states.
- Maintain sufficient contrast for text on paper, ink, and accent surfaces.
